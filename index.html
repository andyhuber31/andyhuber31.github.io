Cisco Home Network Lab

I built a physical Cisco lab to develop hands-on skills in routing, switching, and troubleshooting. It includes Cisco 1921 and 2821 routers and 2960X, 2960, and 3560 switches.

  • VLANs, 802.1Q trunks, and inter-VLAN routing.
  • LACP EtherChannels and spanning-tree configuration.
  • OSPF routing neighbors and HSRP active/standby gateways.
  • Configuration verification and connectivity troubleshooting with Cisco IOS commands.
